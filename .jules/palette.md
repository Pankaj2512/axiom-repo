## 2024-08-07 - Custom Modal Accessibility Standards
**Learning:** Custom portal/overlay modal components in React require manual wiring for screen reader support, unlike native `<dialog>` elements. When implementing custom modals, they often lack explicit ARIA relationships linking the overlay to its title, and close buttons frequently lack contextual labels and focus indicators, leading to a degraded experience for keyboard and screen reader users.
**Action:** Always ensure custom modals include `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and an `aria-labelledby` attribute mapped to a unique ID generated via `React.useId()` on the modal's title element. Furthermore, ensure icon-only close buttons have an explicit `aria-label` and visible focus states (`focus-visible` utilities in Tailwind).
