## 2023-10-05 - Complex Interactive Cards in Next.js Links
**Learning:** When creating complex interactive cards (like ItemCard) that are wrapped in a Next.js `<Link>` component, the outer link absorbs generic focus styles but leaves interactive inner elements (like secondary buttons or toggle icons) inaccessible via keyboard navigation. These inner elements need explicit `aria-label` or `aria-expanded` attributes as well as explicit focus states (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2`) directly applied to them to ensure screen readers and keyboard users can successfully discover and interact with them.
**Action:** When working on interactive cards with inner buttons, always verify that the inner elements have their own explicit ARIA roles, labels, and visible focus rings rather than relying solely on the parent container's generic focus state.
