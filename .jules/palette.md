## 2024-03-24 - Focus States in Next.js Link Wrappers
**Learning:** When complex interactive cards (like `ItemCard`) are wrapped in Next.js `<Link>` tags, explicit focus states (`focus-visible:ring-2`) and ARIA bindings (`aria-label`, `aria-expanded`) must be placed directly on the inner interactive elements (buttons, secondary links) to ensure accessibility, as the parent wrapper's generic focus ring is insufficient. When applying `focus-visible:ring-2` to these inner elements, explicitly pair it with `focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-black` to ensure the focus ring stands out against the background and overrides the generic link focus outline.
**Action:** Always add explicit ARIA labels and `focus-visible` styles with offset to inner interactive elements inside of link wrappers.
