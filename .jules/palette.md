## 2024-10-15 - Floating Action Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Floating Action Buttons (FABs), specifically icon-only ones, are often missing critical context for screen readers and lack proper keyboard focus states, particularly when utilizing Tailwind classes directly. While they may have hover effects or basic styling, they require specific attributes (`aria-label`, `aria-expanded` if they trigger something) and explicit `focus-visible` offset classes (like `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`) to be fully accessible without relying purely on default browser behavior.
**Action:** When implementing or reviewing icon-only FABs, always ensure they are given a dynamic or static `aria-label`, have relevant state-tracking ARIA attributes (like `aria-expanded`), and include explicit `focus-visible:ring-2` styles to ensure proper keyboard navigation visibility.
