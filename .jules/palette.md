## 2024-10-24 - AI Mentor Buttons Accessibility
**Learning:** Floating action buttons and their associated modals often miss proper ARIA labels and focus indicators, making them difficult for screen reader users and keyboard navigators to identify and interact with.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only buttons, especially in floating widgets or modals, have explicit `aria-label` attributes and clear `focus-visible` styles to indicate keyboard focus.
