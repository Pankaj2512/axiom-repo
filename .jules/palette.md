## 2025-02-23 - Focus Accessibility on Custom Floating Action Buttons
**Learning:** When using custom floating action buttons or modal close buttons created with standard `div` or generic `<button>` elements, Next.js or Tailwind focus rings may be occluded by custom backgrounds or generic styles. The explicit `focus-visible:ring-2` with `focus-visible:ring-offset` provides necessary keyboard navigation visibility and requires deliberate implementation. Custom background and overlay styles (like black/60 backdrop) must also have matching offset colors to be visible.
**Action:** When adding accessible floating interactive elements or modals, explicitly verify that both `aria-label`/`aria-expanded` attributes are present and `focus-visible` styling with explicit ring offsets are applied to counteract clipping or background blending.
