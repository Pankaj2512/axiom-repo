## 2024-03-20 - Ensure Explicit Focus States for Nested Link Interactive Elements
**Learning:** When interactive elements (like icon-only buttons or secondary links) are nested inside a generic Next.js `<Link>` wrapper that has its own focus ring (or no clear focus ring), screen readers and keyboard users may not see a clear focus state when tabbing onto the inner interactive elements. The parent `<Link>`'s generic focus ring is often insufficient.
**Action:** Always place explicit `focus-visible:ring-2` (along with correct `focus-visible:ring-offset-*` grounded against the specific background color) and ARIA attributes (e.g., `aria-label`, `aria-expanded`) directly on the inner interactive elements (like buttons or `<a>` tags) inside a `<Link>` wrapper to ensure proper visibility and accessibility.
