## 2024-07-07 - Redundant calculation of totalItems in Tracks
**Learning:** `tracks` in `src/data/tracks.ts` is mostly static data containing `Track`, `Module`, `Topic`, and `Item` hierarchies. Multiple components (`StatsGrid`, `StudyForecaster`, `CategoryProgress`, `ModuleDetailPage`) calculate the total number of items by iterating deeply through these tracks, modules, and topics. This calculation is pure and based on static imported data, but it is recalculated multiple times per component, sometimes within render cycles (`StatsGrid`) or hook effects (`StudyForecaster`).
**Action:** Since the tracks data is statically imported, the total items and similar aggregate statistics can be computed once, memoized, or simply calculated and exported directly alongside `tracks` in the data layer. Next time I notice complex array reductions on static module-level imports, export derived values rather than recalculating in React components.
